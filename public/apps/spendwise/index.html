About Spendwise

A free expense tracker that shows your pace for the month

Spendwise is a simple, private expense tracker. Add what you spend in a few taps and see how this month compares with last month, a daily spending calendar, where your money goes by category, and the charges that repeat every month. Export to CSV whenever you like.

Is Spendwise really free?
Yes. There's no account, sign-up or trial. Open it and start adding expenses.
Where is my spending data stored?
In your own browser on this device. If you tap Save my progress and sign in with your email, a private copy is kept in your GrowDaily account so it comes back on any device. You can export everything to CSV at any time.
What does the monthly pace show?
How much you've spent so far this month compared with the same point last month, and where the month is likely to end if you keep going at the same rate.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. Spendwise works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.
